## Retry-rate definition (locked 2026-05-26)

Criterion #3 counts **redo attempts / total**, where a redo is a duplicate
clean re-run OR an aborted attempt (`error` row). The S1 baseline (~50%: 9
discards + 1 ws_error per 18) was discard-driven; after the Day 4 PTT cooldown
removed double-press discards (0 aborts here), redos surface as deliberate
clean re-runs instead. Counting both keeps the comparison apples-to-apples.
This run: 0 aborts + 10 clean re-runs = 17%, down from S1 ~50%.
